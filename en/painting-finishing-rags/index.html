White for finishing

To apply stain, wipe off excess oil or clean a brush, you need a rag that won't bleed. Our white rags are sorted so they leave no color on wood, metal or fresh paint.

  • White T-shirt: thin and soft, for stain, varnish, polishing and delicate surfaces.
  • White terry: thicker, for spills, tool cleaning and heavy excess.
  • Colored: for job-site cleanup, hands and ladders, at a lower cost.

Who it's for

House painters, woodworkers, furniture and cabinet finishers, furniture restorers, body shops and industrial paint shops.

Careful with stain and oil rags

Rags soaked with linseed oil, tung oil or oil-based stain can catch fire on their own as they dry. Never leave them balled up or in a pile. Lay them flat outdoors to dry, or put them in a closed metal container filled with water. Details in the guide oily rags and fire risk.

Frequently asked questions

T-shirt or terry for staining?

White T-shirt to apply and wipe stain (thin and even), white terry for heavy excess and cleanup.

Can colored rags stain wood?

With solvents, dyed fabric can bleed. For finishing, choose white.

How do I dispose of an oil-stain rag?

Dry it flat outdoors or put it in a closed metal container with water, then take it to your local eco-centre as household hazardous waste.
