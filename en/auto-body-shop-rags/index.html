What body shops use our rags for

  • Degreasing panels before primer, with your usual solvent.
  • Sanding dust and surface cleanup between steps.
  • Spray guns, cups and tools: cleanup after each color.
  • Hands, benches and floors in the shop.

White or colored?

On a surface that's ready for paint, use white T-shirt rags ($42 per 25 lb bag): they leave no dye on the panel. For tools, guns and floors, colored rags at $32 are enough.

Right before the paint coat, most painters use a dedicated tack cloth. Our rags cover every other step.

Solvents and safety

Rags soaked with thinner or solvent belong in a closed metal container. See the guide oily rags and fire risk.

How much does a body shop use?

Usage depends on the number of bays and how much prep work you do. Start with a few 25 lb bags of each type, note how long they last, then use our calculator to plan a monthly order.

Frequently asked questions

Do your rags leave lint on paint?

Sorted white T-shirt rags are low-lint and work well for degreasing. For the last wipe before paint, a dedicated tack cloth is still best.

What should a body shop order?

Many shops keep a mix: a few 25 lb bags of white for surfaces and colored rags for tools and cleanup.
