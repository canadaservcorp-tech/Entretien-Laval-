For the plant and the shop floor

Machine shops, plants, print shops, warehouses and maintenance teams use rags for cutting oil, ink, solvents, machine grease and spills.

  • Printing: white T-shirt to clean presses and rollers with no color transfer.
  • Machining and maintenance: colored terry for oil and grease.
  • General use: colored T-shirt, the most economical.

Volumes and supply

Quantity is unlimited. Outside Laval and Montréal we deliver from 1,000 lb per delivery, with a delivered price stated in the quote. See wholesale rags.

Solvents and used rags

Rags soaked with solvent or oil should be kept in closed metal containers and disposed of under your hazardous materials rules. See the guide oily rags and fire risk.
